Let $A$ be and invertible $n\times n$ matrix. For any $\vec{b}$ in $\large{R^n}$, the unique solution $x$ of $A\vec{x}=b$ has entries given by
$$
\Large{
x_{i}=\frac
{\det (A_{i}(\vec{b}))}
{\det(A)}
}
$$
where $A_{i}(\vec{b})$ is the matrix obtained by replacing the column $i$ by the the vector $\vec{b}$

# Example
Find the values from $s$ for which the system has a solution, and calculate the solution
$$
\Large
\begin{align}
(8-s)x_{1}-2x_{2}  &= 5 \\
4x_{1}+(2-s)x_{2}  &= 3
\end{align}
$$
