# Subspaces  of $R^n$

A Set $\mathcal{W}$ is in a *subspace* of $R^n$ if it satisfies the following properties
1. The zero vector is in $\mathcal{W}$
2. Whenever $u$ and $v$ are in $\mathcal{W}$, then so is $u+v$ (closed under vector addition)
3. Whenever $u$ is in $\mathcal{W}$ and $c$ is a scalar, then  $cu$ is also in $\mathcal{W}$(closed under scalar multiplication)

1. $R^n$ is a subspace of itself
