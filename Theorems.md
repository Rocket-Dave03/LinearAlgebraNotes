# Theorem 14
If a matrix $A$ has $n$ columns, then the [[Rank|rank]] of $A$ plus the [[Dimension|dimension]] of the [[Null Space|null space]] of $A$ is equal to n
$$
\large{rank(A)+dim(Null(A))=n}
$$
